## Reading posture

A project earns detail here when the public-safe evidence is inspectable: README, manifest, schema, test, design doc, branch/status evidence, or explicit caveat. Everything else remains category-level or absent. This is not coyness; it is what keeps a public wiki from becoming a leak with typography.
