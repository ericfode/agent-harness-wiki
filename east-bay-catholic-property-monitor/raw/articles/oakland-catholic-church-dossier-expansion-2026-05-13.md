## Scope

Expanded the watch from the seven Oakland closure sites to all identified Catholic Diocese of Oakland parish/church/pastoral-center sites in Oakland, using the official parish directory plus MAP closure sites that are not ordinary parish-directory entries. This remains Catholic/Diocese-of-Oakland scope, not every Protestant or independent church in Oakland.

## Valuation method

For every site, record Oakland Parcels API values and compute a low-confidence land-centric `agent estimate` from same-ZIP non-Catholic/non-government parcel land-value-per-square-foot medians. See [[valuation-methodology]].
